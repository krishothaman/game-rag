=== #6 What are Prayer Beads?
ANSWER: Prayer Beads are loose items found throughout the game that can be offered at a Sculptor's Idol to increase maximum Vitality and Posture, as stated in excerpt [1]: "Loose prayer beads strung together in offering to a Sculptor's Idol. Increases maximum Vitality and Posture." Additionally, four of these beads can be converted into a Prayer Bead Necklace which also increases those same attributes, according to excerpt [1]: "Four Prayer Beads can be converted at any Sculptor's Idol by selecting to 'Enhance Physical Attributes' into a Prayer Bead Necklace, which increases maximum Vitality and Posture."

=== #11 What happens to Kuro in the Immortal Severance ending?
ANSWER: In the Immortal Severance ending, Wolf must give ONLY the Divine Dragon's Tears to Kuro and stab him in the heart with the Mortal Blade to sever the ties to immortality [3]. Following these events, Wolf forsakes his prosthetic arm and the violence it enables. He returns to the Dilapidated Temple to take up the Sculptor's trade, waiting to aid the next Shinobi seeking strength [3].
