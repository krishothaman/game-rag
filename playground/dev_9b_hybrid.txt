=== #6 What are Prayer Beads?
ANSWER: Prayer Beads are "A loose prayer bead" [1] that, when four of them are offered at a Sculptor's Idol, "will increase maximum Vitality and Posture" [1]. These beads can also be converted into a necklace which "increases maximum Vitality and Posture" [1], and there are "40 Prayer Beads in the game" [1]. Each bead sheds light on character stories, such as the First Prayer Necklace noting that "The ferocity of Ashina's army is renowned throughout the nation" [3] or the Ninth Prayer Necklace describing how "Robert's father came from afar across seas to the south, in search of the Undying" [4].

=== #8 Who is Genichiro Ashina?
ANSWER: Genichiro Ashina is "a character and mandatory boss in which the player is meant to lose" [1]. He is "the current leader of the Ashina Clan" who was "Adopted by the Ashina after the death of his peasant mother" [1]. As "the current leader of the clan and adopted grandson of Isshin," he sought to "kidnap the Divine Heir of the Dragon's Heritage and use the mysterious power of the Dragon's Heritage to gain an upper hand in the upcoming war" [2]. During their second encounter, Genichiro states, "Ashina, this land -- is everything to me. For her sake... I will shed humanity itself" [3].

=== #11 What happens to Kuro in the Immortal Severance ending?
ANSWER: In the Immortal Severance ending, Wolf must "give ONLY the Divine Dragon's Tears to Kuro and stab him in the heart with the Mortal Blade to sever the ties to immortality" [3]. Following this act, Wolf "forsakes his prosthetic arm and the violence it enables" and returns to the Dilapidated Temple [3]. This path is chosen where Wolf agrees that "Lord Kuro need not lose his life" by finding a way where he does not die in the traditional sense of death, but rather ends his immortality through the ritual [2][3].

=== #12 What do you give Kuro to get the Return ending?
ANSWER: To achieve the Return ending, you must give Kuro the Frozen Tears and the Divine Dragon's Tears during the ending [1]. Additionally, you are required to obtain Holy Chapter: Infested [1] and retrieve the dried and fresh fruits of the Serpent for her to get her Frozen Tears [1]. You also need to deliver Rice for Kuro and receive Sweet Rice Balls in return [4], which is given by the Divine Child of Rejuvenation after she is cured of her sickness with a Persimmon or Taro Persimmon [4]. Finally, you must defeat Isshin, the Sword Saint [1] and give Kuro the Frozen Tears and the Divine Dragon's Tears during the ending [1].
