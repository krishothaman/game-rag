=== #102 What's special about the Mortal Blade?
ANSWER: The Mortal Blade is an odachi capable of slaying the undying, and its crimson blade will take the life of any who dares draw it [2]. Without the power of Resurrection, one could not hope to wield this weapon, which allows one to defeat even infested beings [2]. It is required to draw Kuro's blood, one of the ingredients needed to reach the Divine Realm [2]. The blade gives Wolf the ability to sever the ties of immortality on creatures such as True Monk, Headless Ape, Hanbei the Undying, and Retrieving this item [2].

=== #107 How does Dragonrot spread?
