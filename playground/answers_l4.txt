=== #6 What are Prayer Beads?
ANSWER: Prayer Beads are "A loose prayer bead" [1] that serve as an offering to increase maximum Vitality and Posture when given to a Sculptor's Idol. They can be converted into a Prayer Bead Necklace, which also increases maximum Vitality and Posture. In total, there are 40 Prayer Beads in the game.
