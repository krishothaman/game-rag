=== #6 What are Prayer Beads?
ANSWER: Prayer Beads are "A loose prayer bead" [1] that, when four of them are offered at a Sculptor's Idol, "will increase maximum Vitality and Posture" [1]. These beads can also be converted into a necklace which "increases maximum Vitality and Posture" [1], and there are "40 Prayer Beads in the game" [1]. Each bead sheds light on character stories, such as the First Prayer Necklace noting that "The ferocity of Ashina's army is renowned throughout the nation" [3] or the Ninth Prayer Necklace describing how "Robert's father came from afar across seas to the south, in search of the Undying" [4].

=== #8 Who is Genichiro Ashina?
ANSWER: Genichiro Ashina is "a character and mandatory boss in which the player is meant to lose" [1]. He is "the current leader of the Ashina Clan" who was "Adopted by the Ashina after the death of his peasant mother" [1]. As "the current leader of the clan and adopted grandson of Isshin," he sought to "kidnap the Divine Heir of the Dragon's Heritage and use the mysterious power of the Dragon's Heritage to gain an upper hand in the upcoming war" [2]. During their second encounter, Genichiro states, "Ashina, this land -- is everything to me. For her sake... I will shed humanity itself" [3].

=== #11 What happens to Kuro in the Immortal Severance ending?
ANSWER: In the Immortal Severance ending, Wolf must "give ONLY the Divine Dragon's Tears to Kuro and stab him in the heart with the Mortal Blade to sever the ties to immortality" [3]. Following this act, Wolf "forsakes his prosthetic arm and the violence it enables" and returns to the Dilapidated Temple [3]. This path is chosen where Wolf agrees that "Lord Kuro need not lose his life" by finding a way where he does not die in the traditional sense of death, but rather ends his immortality through the ritual [2][3].

=== #19 How do I cure Dragonrot for everyone?
ANSWER: To cure Dragonrot for everyone, you must first give Emma a Dragonrot Blood Sample so she can create a Recovery Charm and a Dragon's Blood Droplet [2]. Once you have these items, the Recovery Charm allows Dragonrot sufferers to be healed from a Sculptor's Idol, while a Dragon's Blood Droplet can be consumed to heal Dragonrot from all those afflicted [3]. Doing so will cause all Rot Essence to disappear, returning the chances of receiving Unseen Aid to the default value [3].

SHOULD: get a Dragonrot Blood Sample and give it to Emma; she makes a cure (Dragon's Blood Droplet)

=== #20 What's the deal with Hanbei at the temple?
ANSWER: Hanbei is a veteran samurai who became an "Infested," a being who cannot perish through normal means [5]. He explains that he has nowhere to go and fits the description of someone who attracts those with nowhere else to go [2]. Hanbei states, "This temple attracts those with nowhere else to go. I certainly fit that description" [3]. He volunteers himself as Wolf's sparring partner because "A warm body that can't die might prove useful to you" [5]. Regarding his time there, he says, "Half to pass the time... And the other half... well... I want to loosen that furrowed brow of yours" [2].

=== #21 What do I do with the Young Lord's Bell Charm?
ANSWER: To use the Young Lord's Bell Charm, you must offer it to Buddha at the Dilapidated Temple, as "Bell charms protect their holders through Buddha's divine protection" [1]. This action is required to unlock the Hirata Estate memory by interacting with the Buddha statue there [1]. If this item isn't retrieved prior to the final invasion of Ashina Castle and Outskirts by the Central Forces, it won't be obtainable for the rest of the playthrough because "the Timid Maid will be found dead" [1].
