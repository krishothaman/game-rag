=== #102 What's special about the Mortal Blade?
ANSWER: The Mortal Blade is an odachi capable of slaying the undying [3]. Its crimson blade will take the life of any who dares draw it [3]. Without the power of Resurrection, one could not hope to wield this weapon, which allows one to defeat even infested beings [3]. It is said that with it, you could kill one who cannot die through normal means [2].

=== #107 How does Dragonrot spread?
ANSWER: Dragonrot spreads when "one with the power of the Dragon's Heritage dies," and "the more one... dies, the more the sickness known as Dragonrot spreads through the world" [1]. It specifically manifests after Wolf "perishes definitely four times" without resurrecting, as "resurrecting does NOT cause Dragonrot" [1]. Additionally, upon dying for the first time in New Game Plus, "Dragonrot will spread" regardless of previous deaths [2]. The illness occurs because "repeated death causes the Dragon's Blood to stagnate," and if this stagnation reaches a point where it "overflows," those without the power become sick [4].
